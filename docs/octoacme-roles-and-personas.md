## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the safe delivery of product changes into production. They align release timing, readiness checks, rollback planning, and stakeholder communication across the broader delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release windows and dependencies with the Project Manager and stakeholders
- Confirm release readiness using CI, QA, security, and operational checks
- Coordinate rollback or mitigation plans when a launch is at risk
- Communicate release status, timing, and risks to relevant stakeholders
- Maintain a clear record of what is included in each release

### Goals
- Deliver changes reliably and with minimal operational disruption
- Reduce release risk and improve predictability
- Ensure all launch requirements are met before production deployment

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch check-ins
- Post-deployment verification updates
- Stakeholder and support notifications for scheduled releases
- Incident and rollback communications when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: aligns release timing, dependencies, and communication plans
- With Product Managers: confirms release scope and launch priorities against roadmap goals
- With Developers: validates that code is ready, deployed correctly, and monitored after release
- With QA / Release Readiness Lead: ensures test evidence, signoff criteria, and defect closure are complete before launch

## QA / Release Readiness Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Release Readiness Leads ensure that quality gates are met before work is accepted for release. They define quality expectations, validate acceptance criteria, and help determine whether a feature is ready to ship.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategy and quality gates for the release
- Validate acceptance criteria and critical user journeys
- Track defects, risk areas, and unresolved quality issues
- Support release decision-making with evidence from testing
- Partner with Engineering and Product on launch readiness

### Goals
- Reduce release defects and customer-facing quality issues
- Improve confidence in feature readiness before deployment
- Ensure quality expectations are clear and measurable

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and risk discussions
- Acceptance criteria validation with Product and Engineering
- Launch signoff updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: provides readiness status and escalates blockers affecting timeline
- With Product Managers: confirms whether business requirements and acceptance criteria are met
- With Developers: collaborates on bug fixes, validation, and test coverage improvements
- With Release Manager: provides signoff evidence and readiness status for deployment

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Partners assess risk, identify security concerns, and ensure that projects meet required controls and compliance expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, workflows, and data handling for security risks
- Identify required controls, policies, and compliance checkpoints
- Recommend mitigations for security and privacy concerns
- Participate in risk review and incident response planning
- Ensure secure practices are included in delivery decisions

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Protect customer data and business trust
- Support safe delivery of features in compliant environments

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and architecture check-ins
- Compliance and risk assessments
- Escalations for urgent issues or vulnerabilities
- Post-incident and mitigation follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: highlights risks that affect timing, scope, or approval gates
- With Product Managers: helps assess trade-offs between speed, risk, and policy requirements
- With Developers: reviews implementation choices, secure coding practices, and remediation steps
- With Release Manager: supports launch gating for security and compliance approvals

## Business Analyst / Customer Advocate

### Role Summary
Business Analysts / Customer Advocates translate stakeholder needs, customer feedback, and operational realities into clear requirements and measurable outcomes. They help keep delivery aligned to customer value and business priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify stakeholder and customer requirements
- Translate business problems into usable requirements and acceptance language
- Identify gaps between user needs and delivery scope
- Support prioritization and trade-off discussions
- Validate that delivered work creates real value

### Goals
- Keep the team grounded in customer value and business outcomes
- Improve clarity of requirements and reduce rework
- Ensure stakeholder expectations are understood and managed

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria and backlog refinement sessions
- Customer feedback synthesis and business update reviews
- Alignment discussions with Product and PM teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: helps clarify scope, dependencies, and stakeholder expectations
- With Product Managers: contributes customer insight and business context for prioritization
- With Developers: ensures requirements are clear, testable, and actionable
- With QA teams: helps define acceptance criteria and validate user value

## Technical Program Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Program Managers / Delivery Leads coordinate cross-functional execution across multiple workstreams, dependencies, and stakeholders. They help teams manage sequencing, risk, and delivery momentum across complex initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate dependencies across teams and initiatives
- Monitor execution progress and identify delivery risks
- Help sequence milestones and cross-team deliverables
- Support decision-making across engineering, product, and operations
- Drive alignment around ownership, timing, and trade-offs

### Goals
- Improve cross-team execution and delivery reliability
- Reduce ambiguity around responsibilities and dependencies
- Keep complex initiatives on track and aligned to roadmap commitments

### Typical Communication
- Cross-functional program updates and dependency reviews
- Milestone and risk tracking with leadership stakeholders
- Weekly cross-team delivery syncs
- Escalation updates for blocked work or unresolved dependencies

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: shares delivery health, risks, and dependency management support
- With Product Managers: aligns roadmap sequencing and priority trade-offs
- With Developers: keeps engineering work coordinated across teams and milestones
- With Release Manager: supports launch readiness across integrated delivery streams
